# Paper reading template for sparse-view 3DGS regime study

For each paper, capture the following five points:

1. Input assumption
   - What input is assumed? (number of views, pose availability, camera setup)

2. Training data
   - What data was used for training? What is the domain shift risk?

3. Evaluation protocol
   - How is quality measured? What metrics and budgets are used?

4. Limitation
   - What does the paper not address or where does it fail?

5. Experiment application
   - How can this paper be incorporated into our regime study?
   - Which of our axes does it affect: view count, overlap, budget, initialization, or refinement?

## Suggested note format

- Paper:
- Core idea:
- Strength:
- Weakness / limitation:
- Relevance to our study:
- Concrete experiment hook:
